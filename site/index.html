Two new IPO filings last week, after a crowded start to October

Week of Mon 19 – Sun 25 Oct 2026 Source: SEBI, Draft Offer Documents filed v1 · built 29 Oct

The five-line note

  1. 2 new DRHPs were filed with SEBI on 19–25 Oct: GAMP Services and Hunger Pangs. The week before had 1 DRHP (Soleos Energy) and 1 updated filing (Paramotor Digital Technology, UDRHP-I).
  2. New filings have slowed since the 5–11 Oct rush. At least 14 DRHPs were filed that week, 11 of them on 5 Oct alone.
  3. Energy is October's clearest theme, based on company names. Inox Clean Energy, Deon Energy, Grace Renewable Energy, Integrum Energy Infrastructure and Soleos Energy all filed DRHPs.
  4. Watch Oravel Stays (OYO's parent company). It filed an addendum to its DRHP on Mon 26 Oct. Tablespace Technologies filed one on 21 Oct. An addendum updates a filing that SEBI is already reviewing.
  5. So what: most of October's pipeline arrived on a single day. Next week's count will show whether 5 Oct was a one-off spike or the start of a busier season.
